# Project Overview

Essie Chops is a **UK-based Nigerian celebration food brand** offering:

* Nigerian pastries
* Puff Puff
* Meat Pies
* Small Chops
* Celebration Cakes
* Birthday Surprise Platters
* Catering
* Comfort Food

The website is **not an e-commerce store** in Version 1.

Its primary purpose is to:

* showcase the brand
* build trust
* display products/services
* encourage enquiries
* convert visitors into WhatsApp conversations

---

# Business Goals

The website should make visitors instantly understand:

* What Essie Chops offers
* Why it's different
* Why they should trust it
* How to enquire or place an order

Primary conversion:

> WhatsApp enquiries

* Contact Form

# Key Takeaways

* The project scope is now well-defined and intentionally limited for V1.
* The design direction has been agreed upon and aligns with the client's goals.
* The generated Figma design provides a strong foundation but needs targeted refinement, especially for mobile responsiveness and component consistency.
* Development should prioritise reusable components and iterative improvements rather than attempting to build everything at once.
* The immediate next step is to finalise pricing and scope with the client before beginning implementation. Once agreed, use the generated React output as a productivity boost, then refactor and polish it into production-quality code.
