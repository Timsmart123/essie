🍰 ESSIE CHOPS — FIGMA MAKE MASTER PROMPT (FINAL V1)
Project Brief

Design a premium, modern, responsive marketing website for Essie Chops, a UK-based Nigerian celebration food brand.

Essie Chops creates handcrafted Nigerian pastries, celebration cakes, birthday surprise platters, authentic comfort food, and catering for birthdays, family gatherings, corporate events, and special occasions.

This is Version 1 of the website.

It is not an ecommerce platform and does not include online checkout or payment processing.

The primary objective is to build trust, showcase the quality of the food and services, and encourage visitors to enquire or place orders through WhatsApp or the contact form.

Design the website as if it were a premium hospitality brand rather than a traditional food ordering website.
